# External Links and Future Monetization

## Current State

Skills Compare uses direct official provider URLs for outbound course links. These links should take users to the provider page where they can verify the latest price, duration, certificate terms, enrollment rules, and course availability.

The product does not currently use affiliate links, referral links, ads, provider partnership claims, commission claims, or monetized redirects.

## Future Monetization Rules

Affiliate or referral links may be added only when they are real, auditable, and tied to a confirmed program. Do not add fake affiliate links, fake tracking parameters, placeholder monetized URLs, or fake discounts.

Provider partnership claims must not appear unless the partnership is confirmed and documented. Commission claims must not appear unless the monetization relationship is real and the disclosure copy has been reviewed.

Disclosure must be visible before or near monetized provider links. Users should still be told that provider details may change and that they must verify price, duration, certificate terms, and enrollment details on the provider page before deciding.

## URL Handling

Current provider URLs should remain direct official URLs until a real monetization program exists. If affiliate or referral URLs are introduced later, a non-affiliate fallback URL should remain available so the catalog can continue to point to the official provider page when monetization is unavailable, paused, or not appropriate.

Do not replace existing provider URLs with unaudited redirects. Do not use link shorteners or external scripts to hide the destination.

## Tracking Expectations

Outbound tracking should continue to distinguish provider CTA context, such as `card`, `detail`, and `compare`. Tracking should describe where the user clicked from; it should not imply a commission, partnership, or conversion event unless that behavior is actually implemented and disclosed.

Do not add analytics vendor logic or external scripts as part of basic CTA standardization.

## Product Checklist

Before adding monetized links, verify that:

- The affiliate or referral URL is real and auditable.
- The direct official provider URL is still available as a fallback.
- Disclosure is visible before or near the monetized CTA.
- No fake discounts, provider partnership claims, or commission claims are present.
- Provider details may change copy remains visible where users make outbound decisions.
- Pricing, certificate terms, duration, enrollment details, and course availability are verified on the provider page.
- Provider CTA tracking still records the correct context.
